Paper backup

Use this if the internet or Wi-Fi fails. Print the cards, cut them out, and hand one face-down to each student. You run the spread on the board using the tracking sheet.

Paper rules (teacher-run)

  1. Setup. Each student gets a card with a secret role, a network group and an age group. Write everyone's name, group and age on the board (not their role!). Link-persons (↔) are also marked on the board.
  2. Fieldwork (about 3 min). Show 3 Chapter 9 questions on the screen (or use any quiz). Each correct answer = 1 token. Innovators secretly write on a slip of paper the names of 2 people they "talk to" and hand it to you.
  3. Spread (you). For each name on an Innovator's slip, roll a die. Same network group: the person adopts the variant on 3–6. A link-person is involved: 4–6. Different group, loose-knit (Market, Campus): 5–6. Different group, close-knit (Harbour, Hillside): 6 only. Also, each earlier adopter passes it to one random member of their own group on a 6. Mark adopters secretly on your tracking sheet. Announce only the total %.
  4. Evidence. Students can trade tokens with you for evidence: 1 token = "Does X use the variant?"; 2 tokens = % of users per network group, or per age group; 3 tokens = "Did X talk to someone outside their network last round?" Write the answer on a slip.
  5. Meeting and vote. Discuss for 3 minutes, then vote by raising hands (or on paper). The person with the most votes is out. Reveal their role.
  6. Winning. Sociolinguists win by voting out all Innovators. Innovators win if 70% of the community uses the variant, or if they survive 5 rounds.
